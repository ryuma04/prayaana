TRIZPYY DESTINATION GUIDE: Bengaluru, Karnataka

Bengaluru: Garden city, royal palaces & craft microbreweries. Karnataka's vibrant capital where royal history meets global innovation. Walk under 100-year-old trees in Cubbon Park, admire the Tudor-style Bangalore Palace, taste legendary butter masala dosas, and experience India's craft beer capital.
Best months: September, October, November, December, January, February, March. Rainy months: July, August.
Typical weather: about 25 degrees C, usually cloudy, 20% chance of rain.
Good for: City Life, Food & Breweries, Heritage, Gardens. Ideal trip length: 2 to 3 days.
Getting there: nearest gateway Kempegowda International Airport (BLR), 35 km. Travelers usually come from Hub.
Local cab for a full day costs about Rs 2,400; an airport transfer about Rs 1,200.
Languages spoken: Kannada, English, Hindi, Tamil.
Highlights: Bangalore Palace tour; Lalbagh Glass House; MTR dosa & filter coffee; Indiranagar brewery crawl; Cubbon Park morning stroll.

AREAS
- Central & British Cantonment: Lunch spot: Mavalli Tiffin Room (MTR), Lalbagh Road (about Rs 350 per person). Dinner: Toit Brewpub, Indiranagar (about Rs 900 per person).
- Palace & Old Pete: Lunch spot: Vidyarthi Bhavan, Gandhi Bazaar (about Rs 250 per person). Dinner: Karavalli, Gateway Hotel (Coastal Dining) (about Rs 1,800 per person).

EXPERIENCES
- Bangalore Palace Royal Tour (Palace & Old Pete). Step into the 1887 Tudor-revival palace of the Wadiyar dynasty with Gothic windows, Victorian furniture, and ornate wooden carvings. It takes about 1 h 30 min, costs Rs 450 per person, is indoors, easy effort, medium walking, best in the morning, and good for kids. Themes: culture, photo, luxury.
- Lalbagh Botanical Gardens & Glass House (Central & British Cantonment). Explore 240 acres of century-old trees, the London-inspired Glass House, and the ancient Lalbagh 3-billion-year-old rock. It takes about 2 h, costs Rs 50 per person, is outdoors, easy effort, medium walking, best in the morning, and good for kids. Themes: nature, photo, relax.
- Old Bangalore Heritage Breakfast & Coffee Trail (Central & British Cantonment). Guided food crawl through iconic South Indian institutions tasting hot crispy Benne Dosas, Kesari Bath, and foamy Mysore filter coffee. It takes about 2 h, costs Rs 800 per person, is outdoors, easy effort, medium walking, best in the morning, and good for kids. Themes: food, culture, social.
- Cubbon Park Stroll & Vidhana Soudha View (Central & British Cantonment). A tranquil walk beneath mahogany and bamboo canopies ending at the neo-Dravidian state legislature building. It takes about 1 h 30 min, costs Rs 0 per person, is outdoors, very easy effort, low walking, best in the afternoon, and good for kids. Themes: nature, photo, relax.
- Indiranagar Craft Microbrewery Experience (Central & British Cantonment). Tasting flight of 6 freshly brewed craft beers paired with Malnad spiced chicken at one of India's pioneer brewpubs. It takes about 2 h, costs Rs 1,200 per person, is indoors, very easy effort, low walking, best in the evening, and not suitable for young kids. Themes: food, social, luxury.
