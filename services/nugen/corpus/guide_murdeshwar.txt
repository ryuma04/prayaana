TRIZPYY DESTINATION GUIDE: Murdeshwar, Karnataka

Murdeshwar: Colossal 123ft seaside Shiva & Netrani scuba island. Towering on the Kanduka Hill peninsula surrounded on three sides by the Arabian Sea. Behold the world's second-tallest Shiva statue and the towering 20-storey Raja Gopuram with high-speed elevator views. Take a thrilling boat expedition to Netrani Island (Pigeon Island) for world-class coral reef scuba diving and snorkeling.
Best months: October, November, December, January, February, March. Rainy months: June, July, August, September.
Typical weather: about 28 degrees C, usually sunny, 20% chance of rain.
Good for: Colossal Shiva, Scuba Diving, Arabian Sea Views, Peninsula Temple. Ideal trip length: 2 days.
Getting there: nearest gateway Mangaluru Airport (IXE), 160 km · Murdeshwar Railway Station, 3 km. Travelers usually come from Bengaluru.
Local cab for a full day costs about Rs 2,300; an airport transfer about Rs 3,800.
Languages spoken: Kannada, Konkani, English, Hindi.
Highlights: 123-foot seaside Lord Shiva statue; Raja Gopuram 18th-floor elevator view; Netrani Island scuba diving & coral reef; Murdeshwar beach sunset & water jet-ski; Bhatkal historic light house excursion.

AREAS
- Temple Peninsula & Beach: Lunch spot: Naveen Beach Restaurant (Over the waves) (about Rs 450 per person). Dinner: RNS Residency Dining Hall (about Rs 600 per person).
- Harbour & Coastal Strip: Lunch spot: Fisherman's Wharf Seaside Shack (about Rs 500 per person). Dinner: Dhenu Atithya Pure Veg (about Rs 300 per person).

EXPERIENCES
- Colossal Seaside Shiva & 20-Storey Gopuram (Temple Peninsula & Beach). Stand at the feet of the towering 123-foot Shiva statue looking out to the sea and take the elevator to the 18th floor of the Raja Gopuram. It takes about 1 h 30 min, costs Rs 20 per person, is outdoors, very easy effort, low walking, best in the morning, and good for kids. Themes: culture, photo, relax.
- Netrani Island Coral Reef Scuba Diving (Harbour & Coastal Strip). Speedboat excursion to Netrani Island for certified scuba diving or guided snorkeling among vibrant coral reefs, parrotfish, and sea turtles. It takes about 4 h, costs Rs 3,800 per person, is outdoors, moderate effort, medium walking, best in the morning, and not suitable for young kids. Themes: adventure, nature.
- Murdeshwar Peninsula Beach & Water Jet-Ski (Temple Peninsula & Beach). Golden sand beach adjacent to the temple offering jet-ski rides, banana boats, and fresh coconut water beneath swaying palms. It takes about 1 h 30 min, costs Rs 400 per person, is outdoors, easy effort, low walking, best in the evening, and good for kids. Themes: adventure, relax, social.

STAYS
- RNS Residency Sea View in Beach Peninsula: boutique stay, Rs 4,200 per room per night. Amenities: Full Sea View Balconies, Adjacent to Shiva Statue, Seafood Restaurant.
