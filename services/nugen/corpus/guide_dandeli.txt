TRIZPYY DESTINATION GUIDE: Dandeli, Karnataka

Dandeli: White water rapids, hornbill canopy walks & wilderness camps. The adventure capital of Karnataka in the lush deciduous forests of the Western Ghats. Ride Grade 3 rapids on the turbulent Kali River, spot all four South Indian hornbill species on jungle canopy walks, explore ancient limestone Kavala Caves, and kayak Supa Reservoir.
Best months: October, November, December, January, February, March, April, May. Rainy months: June, July, August, September.
Typical weather: about 26 degrees C, usually sunny, 20% chance of rain.
Good for: River Rafting, Hornbill Birds, Adventure Sports, Dense Jungle. Ideal trip length: 3 days.
Getting there: nearest gateway Hubballi Airport (HBX), 75 km · Alnavar Junction, 32 km. Travelers usually come from Bengaluru.
Local cab for a full day costs about Rs 2,600; an airport transfer about Rs 2,500.
Languages spoken: Kannada, Marathi, Hindi, English.
Highlights: Grade 3 Kali River white water rafting; Timber Trail canopy hornbill walk; Syntheri Rocks monolithic granite gorge; Kavala Caves limestone exploration; Kayaking on Supa Reservoir backwaters.

AREAS
- Kali River & Rafting Stretch: Lunch spot: Kali Adventure Camp Riverside Buffet (about Rs 650 per person). Dinner: Hornbill River Resort Barbecue (about Rs 850 per person).
- Kulgi & Syntheri Rocks: Lunch spot: Wildwoods Nature Canteen (about Rs 350 per person). Dinner: Dandeli Jungle Inn Dining (about Rs 500 per person).

EXPERIENCES
- Kali River Grade 3 White Water Rafting (Kali River & Rafting Stretch). Tackle 9 thrilling rapids along 12 kilometres of the gushing Kali River surrounded by dense Western Ghats rainforest. It takes about 3 h, costs Rs 1,600 per person, is outdoors, tiring effort, medium walking, best in the morning, and not suitable for young kids. Themes: adventure, nature.
- Syntheri Rocks Monolithic Granite Gorge Walk (Kulgi & Syntheri Rocks). Walk down into a 300-foot natural limestone and granite ravine carved over eons by the fast-flowing Kaneri River. It takes about 2 h, costs Rs 40 per person, is outdoors, moderate effort, high walking, best in the afternoon, and good for kids. Themes: nature, photo.
- Jungle Canopy Walk & Hornbill Birding (Kali River & Rafting Stretch). Walk suspended 30 feet in the forest tree tops spotting Great Indian Hornbills, Malabar pied hornbills, and emerald doves. It takes about 1 h 30 min, costs Rs 350 per person, is outdoors, easy effort, medium walking, best in the morning, and good for kids. Themes: nature, photo.

STAYS
- Kali Adventure Camp in River Kali Bank: boutique stay, Rs 5,800 per room per night. Amenities: River Tents & Rooms, Rafting Desk, Coracle Ride, Campfire Buffet.

GETTING THERE
- Hubballi Airport to Dandeli Jungle Cab (car) from Hubballi: Rs 900 per person, about 1 h 30 min, departs 10:00.
