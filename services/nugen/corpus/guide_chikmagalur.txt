TRIZPYY DESTINATION GUIDE: Chikmagalur, Karnataka

Chikmagalur: Karnataka's highest peaks & the birthplace of Indian coffee. Nestled in the Baba Budan range where coffee was first planted in India. Summit Mullayanagiri (1,930m) for panoramic 360-degree mountain vistas, explore sacred Baba Budangiri caves, take an adventurous 4x4 jeep ride to Hebbe Falls, and stay in heritage coffee planter bungalows.
Best months: September, October, November, December, January, February, March, April. Rainy months: June, July, August.
Typical weather: about 21 degrees C, usually cloudy, 25% chance of rain.
Good for: High Peaks, Coffee Estates, Trekking, Jeep Safaris. Ideal trip length: 3 days.
Getting there: nearest gateway Mangaluru Airport (IXE), 150 km · Bengaluru, 240 km. Travelers usually come from Bengaluru.
Local cab for a full day costs about Rs 2,800; an airport transfer about Rs 4,500.
Languages spoken: Kannada, English, Hindi.
Highlights: Mullayanagiri sunrise peak trek; Baba Budangiri & Manikyadhara falls; Hebbe Falls 4x4 jungle drive; Coffee Museum cupping experience; Bhadra wildlife sanctuary buffer tour.

AREAS
- Chikmagalur Town & Lower Estates: Lunch spot: Town Canteen (Crispy Gulbarga dosas) (about Rs 200 per person). Dinner: Siri Cafe & Planters Dining (about Rs 550 per person).
- Mullayanagiri & Baba Budan Hills: Lunch spot: Hilltop Estate Planter's Lunch (about Rs 450 per person). Dinner: The Serai Coffee Restaurant (about Rs 1,200 per person).

EXPERIENCES
- Mullayanagiri Peak Sunrise Summit Trek (Mullayanagiri & Baba Budan Hills). Hike Karnataka's highest peak (1,930m) across grassy mountain ridges with panoramic 360-degree views above morning clouds. It takes about 2 h 30 min, costs Rs 0 per person, is outdoors, tiring effort, high walking, best in the morning, and not suitable for young kids. Themes: adventure, nature, photo.
- Baba Budangiri & Manikyadhara Falls (Mullayanagiri & Baba Budan Hills). Sacred mountain shrine where Sufi saint Baba Budan first planted coffee beans brought from Yemen, and cool stream falls. It takes about 2 h, costs Rs 0 per person, is outdoors, moderate effort, medium walking, best in the morning, and good for kids. Themes: culture, nature.
- Hebbe Falls 4x4 Mountain Jeep Safari (Mullayanagiri & Baba Budan Hills). A rugged 4-wheel drive journey through dense coffee valleys followed by a short trek to an untamed twin waterfall. It takes about 3 h, costs Rs 1,800 per person, is outdoors, moderate effort, medium walking, best in the afternoon, and not suitable for young kids. Themes: adventure, nature.
- Coffee Board Museum & Cupping Workshop (Chikmagalur Town & Lower Estates). Interactive museum documenting the botany, history, and roasting methods of Indian coffee with sensory cupping evaluation. It takes about 1 h 30 min, costs Rs 50 per person, is indoors, very easy effort, low walking, best in the afternoon, and good for kids. Themes: food, culture.
- Bhadra Wildlife Sanctuary Buffer Safari (Chikmagalur Town & Lower Estates). Evening jeep safari through lush moist deciduous teak forests scouting for leopards, barking deer, and Malabar giant squirrels. It takes about 3 h, costs Rs 900 per person, is outdoors, easy effort, low walking, best in the evening, and good for kids. Themes: nature, adventure, photo.

STAYS
- The Serai Chikmagalur in Mugthihalli: luxury stay, Rs 19,000 per room per night. Amenities: Private Pool Villas, Coffee Bean Spa, Plantation Biking, Lounge.
- Mullayanagiri Mist Homestay in Foothills: homestay stay, Rs 2,800 per room per night. Amenities: Peak View, Malnad Thali, Estate Trail, Bonfire.

GETTING THERE
