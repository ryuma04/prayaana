TRIZPYY DESTINATION GUIDE: Coorg, Karnataka

Coorg: Coffee hills, cascading waterfalls & misty plantations. The Scotland of India in the Western Ghats. Walk through fragrant coffee and black pepper estates, bathe elephants at Dubare on the Kaveri river, marvel at the Golden Temple in Bylakuppe Tibetan settlement, and watch rolling clouds from Raja's Seat.
Best months: October, November, December, January, February, March, April, May. Rainy months: June, July, August, September.
Typical weather: about 22 degrees C, usually cloudy, 30% chance of rain.
Good for: Coffee Country, Misty Hills, Waterfalls, Homestays. Ideal trip length: 3 to 4 days.
Getting there: nearest gateway Kannur Airport (CNN), 90 km · Mangaluru Airport (IXE), 140 km. Travelers usually come from Bengaluru.
Local cab for a full day costs about Rs 2,600; an airport transfer about Rs 4,200.
Languages spoken: Kannada, Kodava, English.
Highlights: Coffee plantation walking & cupping; Dubare Elephant Camp Kaveri bath; Abbey Falls & hanging bridge; Namdroling Golden Temple; Raja's Seat sunset over valleys.

AREAS
- Madikeri Town & Hills: Lunch spot: Coorg Cuisine, Madikeri (about Rs 420 per person). Dinner: Raintree Heritage Restaurant (about Rs 650 per person).
- Kushalnagar & Bylakuppe: Lunch spot: Tibetan Camp 4 Canteen, Bylakuppe (about Rs 280 per person). Dinner: Green Haven Multi-Cuisine (about Rs 450 per person).

EXPERIENCES
- Private Coffee & Pepper Estate Guided Walk (Madikeri Town & Hills). Follow the bean from shade-grown Arabica bush to roasting drum, smelling fresh pepper vines and tasting estate-pressed brew. It takes about 2 h, costs Rs 750 per person, is outdoors, easy effort, medium walking, best in the morning, and good for kids. Themes: nature, food, relax.
- Abbey Falls Trail & Hanging Bridge (Madikeri Town & Hills). Short descent through lush coffee and spice bushes to a roaring waterfall framed by the hanging footbridge. It takes about 1 h 15 min, costs Rs 30 per person, is outdoors, easy effort, medium walking, best in the morning, and good for kids. Themes: nature, photo.
- Dubare Elephant Camp River Crossing & Bath (Kushalnagar & Bylakuppe). Cross Kaveri waters by ferry to groom, feed, and interact with gentle elephants alongside trained tribal mahouts. It takes about 2 h 30 min, costs Rs 600 per person, is outdoors, easy effort, low walking, best in the morning, and good for kids. Themes: nature, adventure.
- Namdroling Golden Temple Bylakuppe (Kushalnagar & Bylakuppe). Step into the serenity of India's largest Tibetan monastic settlement featuring three 40-foot gilded golden Buddha statues. It takes about 1 h 30 min, costs Rs 0 per person, is indoors, very easy effort, low walking, best in the afternoon, and good for kids. Themes: culture, relax.
- Sunset over Western Ghats from Raja's Seat (Madikeri Town & Hills). The royal garden cliff where Kodava monarchs spent evenings admiring crimson sunsets over misty undulating valleys. It takes about 1 h, costs Rs 25 per person, is outdoors, very easy effort, low walking, best in the evening, and good for kids. Themes: relax, photo.

STAYS

- The Tamara Coorg in Kabbinakad Estate: luxury stay, Rs 24,000 per room per night. Amenities: Stilted Cottages, Coffee Plantation, Elevation Spa, Fine Dining.
- Club Mahindra Madikeri Resort in Galibeedu Road: boutique stay, Rs 7,500 per room per night. Amenities: Valley View, Two Swimming Pools, Kids Activity Zone, Rainforest Trek.
