TRIZPYY DESTINATION GUIDE: Belur & Halebidu, Karnataka

Belur & Halebidu: UNESCO World Heritage Hoysala soapstone temple masterpieces. The 12th-century pinnacle of medieval Indian architecture. In Belur, stand awestruck by Chennakeshava Temple's dancing bracket figures carved from soft chloritic schist. In Halebidu, study the twin Hoysaleswara temples adorned with 20,000 intricately carved animals, battles, and mythological epics.
Best months: September, October, November, December, January, February, March. Rainy months: July, August.
Typical weather: about 25 degrees C, usually sunny, 15% chance of rain.
Good for: Hoysala Masterpieces, Soapstone Carvings, UNESCO Heritage, Medieval Art. Ideal trip length: 1 to 2 days.
Getting there: nearest gateway Mangaluru Airport (IXE), 150 km · Hassan Junction, 35 km. Travelers usually come from Bengaluru.
Local cab for a full day costs about Rs 2,400; an airport transfer about Rs 3,500.
Languages spoken: Kannada, English, Hindi.
Highlights: Belur Chennakeshava Temple bracket figures; Halebidu Hoysaleswara temple friezes; Kedareshwara Temple star-shaped plinth; Jain Basadi complex Halebidu; Yagachi Dam water sports break.

- Halebidu & Hassan Junction: Lunch spot: Mayura Shantala Halebidu (about Rs 300 per person). Dinner: Hassan Ashok Royal Kitchen (about Rs 750 per person).

EXPERIENCES
- Belur Chennakeshava Hoysala Temple Masterpiece (Belur Chennakeshava Quarter). Commissioned in 1117 CE, home to 42 celestial maiden (Madanika) bracket figures sculpted in breathless lace-like soapstone details. It takes about 2 h, costs Rs 0 per person, is outdoors, easy effort, medium walking, best in the morning, and good for kids. Themes: culture, photo.
- Halebidu Hoysaleswara Twin Temple Friezes (Halebidu & Hassan Junction). Twin temples on star plinths enveloped by eight outer sculptural bands depicting 1,248 battle elephants, lions, horses, and Mahabharata scenes. It takes about 2 h, costs Rs 25 per person, is outdoors, easy effort, medium walking, best in the afternoon, and good for kids. Themes: culture, photo.
