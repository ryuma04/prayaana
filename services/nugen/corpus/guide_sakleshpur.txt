TRIZPYY DESTINATION GUIDE: Sakleshpur, Karnataka

Sakleshpur: Star fort, green railway trek & cardamom hills. A tranquil hill station in the Western Ghats famous for aromatic spice hills and mountain vistas. Climb the 8-pointed French-designed Manjarabad Star Fort built by Tipu Sultan, marvel at the sweeping Bisle Ghat reserve view of three mountain ranges, and trek through private cardamom plantations.
Best months: September, October, November, December, January, February, March, April. Rainy months: June, July, August.
Typical weather: about 22 degrees C, usually cloudy, 25% chance of rain.
Good for: Star Fort, Cardamom Hills, Western Ghats Ridge, Homestays. Ideal trip length: 2 to 3 days.
Getting there: nearest gateway Mangaluru Airport (IXE), 130 km · Sakleshpur Railway Station, 2 km. Travelers usually come from Bengaluru.
Local cab for a full day costs about Rs 2,600; an airport transfer about Rs 3,800.
Languages spoken: Kannada, English.
Highlights: Manjarabad 8-point Star Fort trek; Bisle Ghat panoramic ridge viewpoint; Jenukallu Gudda peak hike; Cardamom estate guided tasting; Magajahalli Waterfalls natural bath.

AREAS
- Sakleshpur Town & Fort: Lunch spot: Surabhi Restaurant, Town Centre (about Rs 250 per person). Dinner: Mythri Heritage Dining (about Rs 450 per person).
- Bisle & Plantation Valleys: Lunch spot: Planter's Home Dining (Malnad style) (about Rs 400 per person). Dinner: Eka Resort Valley Dining (about Rs 750 per person).

EXPERIENCES
- Manjarabad Eight-Point Star Fort Hike (Sakleshpur Town & Fort). Trek up 250 stone steps to explore the unique star-shaped laterite military fort built by Tipu Sultan in 1792 with secret tunnels. It takes about 2 h, costs Rs 20 per person, is outdoors, moderate effort, high walking, best in the morning, and good for kids. Themes: culture, adventure, photo.
- Bisle Ghat Panoramic Mountain Ridge View (Bisle & Plantation Valleys). Stand on the glass-railed observation platform viewing the virgin rainforest valley of three mountain ranges: Kumara Parvatha, Pushpagiri, and Dodda Betta. It takes about 2 h 30 min, costs Rs 50 per person, is outdoors, easy effort, low walking, best in the morning, and good for kids. Themes: nature, photo, relax.

STAYS
- Eka Resort Sakleshpur in Halli Bailu: boutique stay, Rs 5,200 per room per night. Amenities: Lake View Cottages, Plantation Trail, Boating, Bonfire.
