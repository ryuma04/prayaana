TRIZPYY DESTINATION GUIDE: Udupi, Karnataka

Udupi: Sacred Krishna temple, basalt sea islands & vegetarian heritage. The birthplace of world-renowned Udupi vegetarian cuisine. Pay homage through Kanakana Kindi at Sri Krishna Matha, hop a ferry to St. Mary's Island with its million-year-old hexagonal basalt columns, walk Malpe Beach's sea-walk, and climb Kaup lighthouse at dusk.
Best months: October, November, December, January, February, March. Rainy months: June, July, August, September.
Typical weather: about 28 degrees C, usually sunny, 20% chance of rain.
Good for: Temple Town, Coastal Islands, Udupi Cuisine, Sunsets. Ideal trip length: 2 to 3 days.
Getting there: nearest gateway Mangaluru International Airport (IXE), 55 km · Udupi Railway Station, 4 km. Travelers usually come from Bengaluru.
Local cab for a full day costs about Rs 2,200; an airport transfer about Rs 1,800.
Languages spoken: Kannada, Tulu, Konkani, English.
Highlights: Sri Krishna Matha & Kanakana Kindi; St. Mary's Island hexagonal rocks; Malpe Sea Walk & watersports; Mitra Samaj Goli Baje & Masala Dosa; Kaup Lighthouse sunset vista.

AREAS
- Krishna Matha & Town: Lunch spot: Mitra Samaj (Iconic 1949 eatery) (about Rs 180 per person). Dinner: Woodlands Restaurant, Udupi (about Rs 320 per person).
- Malpe Beach & Coastal Strip: Lunch spot: Fish Factory Malpe (Fresh Coastal Catch) (about Rs 550 per person). Dinner: Thamboolam Coastal Dining (about Rs 650 per person).

EXPERIENCES
- Sri Krishna Matha & Kanakana Kindi Darshan (Krishna Matha & Town). Visit the sacred 13th-century monastery founded by saint Madhvacharya, viewing the silver-clad deity through the nine-hole window. It takes about 1 h 30 min, costs Rs 0 per person, is indoors, very easy effort, low walking, best in the morning, and good for kids. Themes: culture, relax.
- St. Mary's Island Basalt Rock Boat Expedition (Malpe Beach & Coastal Strip). Take a scenic boat ride to uninhabited islands featuring unique millions-year-old hexagonal volcanic columnar basalt rock formations. It takes about 2 h 30 min, costs Rs 450 per person, is outdoors, easy effort, medium walking, best in the morning, and good for kids. Themes: nature, adventure, photo.
- Authentic Udupi Tiffin & Filter Coffee Trail (Krishna Matha & Town). Taste traditional Goli Baje, Tuppa Dosa, Patrode, and Halbai at heritage Brahmin culinary establishments. It takes about 1 h 30 min, costs Rs 350 per person, is indoors, very easy effort, low walking, best in the afternoon, and good for kids. Themes: food, culture.
- Kaup Beach & 1901 British Lighthouse Climb (Malpe Beach & Coastal Strip). Climb the spiral staircase of the 27-metre operational lighthouse perched on granite rocks for sweeping Arabian Sea views at sunset. It takes about 1 h 30 min, costs Rs 30 per person, is outdoors, easy effort, medium walking, best in the evening, and good for kids. Themes: photo, relax, nature.
