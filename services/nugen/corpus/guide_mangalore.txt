TRIZPYY DESTINATION GUIDE: Mangalore, Karnataka

Mangalore: Coastal city of ghee roast, historic cathedrals & surf beaches. The gateway to Coastal Karnataka where Arabian Sea culture thrives. Taste legendary Chicken Ghee Roast where it was invented, marvel at St. Aloysius Chapel's Italian-painted frescoes, learn surfing at Sasihithlu, and tour the historic 10th-century Mangaladevi Temple.
Best months: October, November, December, January, February, March. Rainy months: June, July, August, September.
Typical weather: about 29 degrees C, usually sunny, 20% chance of rain.
Good for: Coastal Feast, Heritage Churches, Surfing & Sun, Culture. Ideal trip length: 2 days.
Getting there: nearest gateway Mangaluru International Airport (IXE), 12 km · Mangalore Central Station. Travelers usually come from Bengaluru.
Local cab for a full day costs about Rs 2,200; an airport transfer about Rs 1,200.
Languages spoken: Kannada, Tulu, Konkani, Beary, English.
Highlights: Authentic Chicken & Paneer Ghee Roast; St. Aloysius Chapel ceiling paintings; Panambur Beach sunset & water sports; Kudroli Gokarnanatheshwara Temple; Pabbas Ice Cream Gudbud special.

AREAS
- City Centre & Hampankatta: Lunch spot: Giri Manja's Seafood Shack (about Rs 450 per person). Dinner: Maharaja Restaurant (Ghee Roast specialist) (about Rs 750 per person).
- Panambur & Tannirbhavi Beaches: Lunch spot: Ideal Ice Cream Cafe / Pabbas (about Rs 250 per person). Dinner: Gajalee Sea Food Restaurant (about Rs 900 per person).

EXPERIENCES
- Origins of Ghee Roast: Mangalorean Culinary Trail (City Centre & Hampankatta). A mouthwatering food journey tasting authentic fiery Kundapura Ghee Roast, Anjal (Kingfish) Tava Fry, Neer Dosa, and Kori Rotti. It takes about 2 h, costs Rs 850 per person, is indoors, very easy effort, low walking, best in the afternoon, and good for kids. Themes: food, social.
- St. Aloysius Chapel Frescoes & Heritage Walk (City Centre & Hampankatta). Admire the Sistine Chapel of India, decorated in 1899 by Italian Jesuit painter Antony Moscheni with breathtaking ceiling frescoes. It takes about 1 h 15 min, costs Rs 0 per person, is indoors, very easy effort, low walking, best in the morning, and good for kids. Themes: culture, photo.
- Panambur Beach Sunset & Coastal Watersports (Panambur & Tannirbhavi Beaches). Jet-skiing, camel rides, and golden sand sunset watching on Karnataka's most vibrant and safest guarded coastal beach. It takes about 2 h, costs Rs 350 per person, is outdoors, easy effort, medium walking, best in the evening, and good for kids. Themes: adventure, relax, social.

STAYS
