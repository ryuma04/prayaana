TRIZPYY DESTINATION GUIDE: Bandipur, Karnataka

Bandipur: Premier Project Tiger reserve in the Nilgiri Biosphere. A dramatic deciduous forest where the Western and Eastern Ghats meet. Home to majestic tigers, leopards, dholes (wild dogs), and gaur. Ascend Himavad Gopalaswamy Betta shrouded in mountain mist, and join morning and evening forest department gypsy safaris.
Best months: October, November, December, January, February, March, April, May. Rainy months: July, August.
Typical weather: about 25 degrees C, usually sunny, 15% chance of rain.
Good for: Project Tiger, Forest Safaris, Mist Viewpoint, Wildlife. Ideal trip length: 2 days.
Getting there: nearest gateway Mysore Airport, 70 km · Bengaluru Airport, 220 km. Travelers usually come from Bengaluru.
Local cab for a full day costs about Rs 2,800; an airport transfer about Rs 3,800.
Languages spoken: Kannada, Tamil, English.
Highlights: Bandipur Tiger Reserve safari; Himavad Gopalaswamy Betta temple & mist view; Gaur (Indian Bison) herd tracking; Jungle night nature sounds walk; Elephant rehabilitation camp visit.

AREAS
- Bandipur Safari Gate & NH766: Lunch spot: Bandipur Safari Lodge Buffet (about Rs 650 per person). Dinner: Windflower Tusker Bar & Restaurant (about Rs 1,100 per person).
- Gundlupet & Foothills: Lunch spot: Highway Green Dhaba (about Rs 300 per person). Dinner: Country Club Bandipur Restaurant (about Rs 600 per person).

EXPERIENCES
- Bandipur Tiger Reserve Morning Jeep Safari (Bandipur Safari Gate & NH766). Early dawn safari tracking royal Bengal tigers, sloth bears, Indian gaur (bison), and crested serpent eagles across dry deciduous forest. It takes about 2 h 30 min, costs Rs 1,500 per person, is outdoors, easy effort, low walking, best in the morning, and good for kids. Themes: nature, adventure, photo.
- Himavad Gopalaswamy Betta Misty Peak (Gundlupet & Foothills). Ascend to the highest peak in Bandipur National Park (1,450m) covered in year-round fog, featuring a 14th-century temple and elephant herds. It takes about 2 h, costs Rs 200 per person, is outdoors, easy effort, low walking, best in the morning, and good for kids. Themes: nature, culture, photo.

STAYS
- Bandipur Safari Lodge (Jungle Lodges) in National Park Boundary: boutique stay, Rs 7,500 per room per night. Amenities: Daily Safaris Included, Cottages, Buffet Meals, Birding Walk.

GETTING THERE
